# Sponsoring & Investing in Emris AI Desktop

Thank you for your interest in supporting **Emris AI Desktop**!

Emris AI Desktop is an open-source, ultra-lightweight AI engineering workstation combining **Python** and **React** via native OS webviews (`pywebview`). Our goal is to provide developers worldwide with an autonomous, privacy-centric, and blazing-fast pair-programming workstation without the resource bloat and memory footprint of traditional Electron applications.

---

## Why Support Emris AI?

1. **Next-Generation Lightweight Architecture**: While modern AI code editors demand 500MB–1GB+ of RAM running Chromium runtimes, Emris AI runs on a ~15 MB native shell that consumes less than 150 MB of memory.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Agent Workflows**: Designed from the ground up for autonomous task decomposition, line-bounded code synthesis, virtualenv isolation, and real-time failure diagnostics.
3. **Open-Source & Community-First**: We believe developer tooling should be transparent, extensible, and accessible to engineers everywhere.
